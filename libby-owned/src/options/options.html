    Accepted formats

    CSV (comma, semicolon or tab separated) with a header row, or JSON: an array of objects. Column/key names are matched loosely:

    • Title (required): title, book title, name
    • Author: author, authors, written by, additional authors
    • Narrator: narrator, narrators, narrated by, read by
    • Format(s) (optional): format, formats, type, binding. A list (["ebook","audio"] or ebook; audio) means you own several formats. Recognized: audio / ebook (kindle, epub…) / physical (print, paperback, hardcover…).
    • Status (optional, shown in the tooltip): status, shelf

    Without a format column, rows with a narrator are audiobooks and the rest are books of unspecified format (treated like ebooks). Separate multiple people with ;, &, and or commas.

    title,author,narrator,format
Project Hail Mary,Andy Weir,Ray Porter,
The Name of the Wind,Patrick Rothfuss,,ebook
